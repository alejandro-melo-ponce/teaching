# ECON101 — Handouts and Study Guides

I prepared these materials to support practice and further exploration alongside my lectures.

## Midterm II study guide — Fall 2019

I designed this study guide to help students prepare for the open-problem portion of Midterm II. It covers taxes and subsidies, public goods, externalities, and monopoly. I encourage students to work through the problems both graphically and algebraically. The accompanying solutions are concise and do not include the graphs.

- [Study guide](ECON101_Midterm-II-Study-Guide_Fall2019.pdf)
- [Suggested solutions](ECON101_Midterm-II-Study-Guide-Solutions_Fall2019.pdf)

## A Giffen Example — Fall 2020

I originally developed this exercise for an intermediate microeconomics course I taught as a graduate student, drawing inspiration from Haagsma (2012), as acknowledged in the handout. I shared it in ECON101 as an optional extension for students interested in Giffen behavior. A full treatment uses multivariable calculus, but students can also explore the example by graphing demand with the supplied parameters.

- [A Giffen Example](ECON101_Giffen-Example_Fall2020.pdf)
