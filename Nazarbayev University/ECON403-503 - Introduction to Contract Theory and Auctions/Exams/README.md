## Acknowledgments

I am grateful to my colleague **Prof. Levent Koçkesen**, whose lecture notes on information economics were an important resource in developing my notes on screening and moral hazard in principal–agent models for the 2019–2021 offerings.

For the course from 2022 onward, I drew on **Omer Tamuz's game-theory lecture notes**, **Michael Maschler, Eilon Solan, and Shmuel Zamir's *Game Theory***, and **Vijay Krishna's *Auction Theory***. These works provided a foundation for the course's organization and influenced its style of exposition. The selection of topics, depth of coverage, and emphasis reflect my own approach to teaching the course.
