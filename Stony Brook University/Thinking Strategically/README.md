# ECO364 — Thinking Strategically

**Stony Brook University · Undergraduate · Online · Summers 2017, 2018, and 2019**  
**Instructor:** Alejandro Melo Ponce

## Course development and acknowledgment

I developed the online version of ECO364 — Thinking Strategically through the **2017 Summer Online Teaching Initiative Scholarship**, awarded by the **Office of the Provost at Stony Brook University**.

I based the online course on the face-to-face version taught in previous years by **Professors Yair Tauman and Roberto Burguet**, and drew on their teaching materials in preparing the slides shared here.

I taught the online course in the summers of **2017, 2018, and 2019**. I delivered additional course activities through **Blackboard**, the university's learning management system. Here I share the syllabus and lecture notes.
