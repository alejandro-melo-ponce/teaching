# ECO321 — Econometrics

**Stony Brook University · Undergraduate · Spring 2017 and Spring 2018**  
**Instructor:** Alejandro Melo Ponce

I taught this course in Spring 2017 and Spring 2018, introducing statistical techniques used in applied econometrics and motivating theory through real-world applications. Topics include probability and statistics, ordinary least squares estimation, hypothesis testing, multiple regression, nonlinear regression functions, and model specification and evaluation.

Here I share my Spring 2018 syllabus, lecture notes, assignments and solutions, along with selected retired exams from both years. I used R for the empirical assignments.

## Prerequisites

In Spring 2018, I required a grade of C or higher in ECO320 or AMS310, and a grade of C or higher in ECO108.

## Course references

I used the following principal textbook:

- James H. Stock and Mark W. Watson. *Introduction to Econometrics*. 3rd edition. Pearson, 2011. I used MyLab Economics with the Pearson eText.

I also recommended:

- Jeffrey M. Wooldridge. *Introductory Econometrics: A Modern Approach*. 6th edition. Cengage Learning, 2016.
- Joshua D. Angrist and Jörn-Steffen Pischke. *Mastering ’Metrics: The Path from Cause to Effect*. Princeton University Press, 2014.
