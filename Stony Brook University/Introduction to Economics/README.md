# ECO108 — Introduction to Economics

**Stony Brook University · Undergraduate · Summer 2015**  
**Instructor:** Alejandro Melo Ponce

I taught this course in Summer 2015, introducing analytical tools for understanding market economies and evaluating economic policy. Topics include supply and demand, consumer choice, firm theory, national income accounting, inflation, and money and banking.

## Textbook and acknowledgment

I based my lecture slides, assignments, and exams on the course textbook:

Roger LeRoy Miller. *Economics Today*. Customized version for ECO108. Pearson, 2015.
